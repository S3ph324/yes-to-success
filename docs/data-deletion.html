Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 15, 2026

The John Calub Content Bot is an internal-use tool used solely by authorized administrators of the John Calub Facebook page. The only personal data stored is the access token of the page administrator who connected the App.

To request deletion of your data

Option A — Revoke from Facebook directly (fastest)

  1. Go to your Facebook account: https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools
  2. Find John Calub Content Bot in the list of connected apps
  3. Click Remove. The App's access token is invalidated immediately by Facebook.

Option B — Request deletion from us

Send an email to jackinternetyt324@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • The Facebook page name and ID
  • The email address associated with the page admin account
  • Confirmation that you are an authorized administrator of the page

We will delete all stored tokens, post records, and configuration associated with the requesting page within 30 days of receiving the request and confirm via reply email when deletion is complete.

What gets deleted

  • Stored Facebook access token(s) for the page
  • Page ID and configured metadata (signoff, brand colors, etc)
  • Records of previously scheduled and posted content created through the App
  • Any uploaded brand assets (logos, character reference photos)

What does NOT get deleted

Published Facebook posts themselves remain on the Facebook page after revocation; they are owned by the page itself, not by the App. To delete those, manage the posts directly within Facebook.
