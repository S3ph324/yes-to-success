Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the John Calub Content Bot (the "App") collects, uses, and stores information when authorized administrators of the John Calub Facebook page use it to manage content publishing on that page.

This App is an internal-use tool operated by the John Calub Training International team. It is not available for public install, account signup, or end-user use. Only authorized team members granted access by John Calub Training International may use this App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 From Facebook (via the Meta Graph API)

When an authorized administrator connects the App to a Facebook Page, the App stores:

  • Facebook Page Access Token — used to publish content on behalf of the page
  • Facebook Page ID and name — to identify the target page for publishing
  • Page follower count — for display only, to confirm the correct page is connected
  • Post IDs of published content — to track which scheduled posts were successfully published

The App does not collect:

  • Profile data of page followers or visitors
  • Messages sent to the page
  • Comments left on the page's posts
  • Any information from any other Facebook user except the page administrator who authorizes the App

1.2 Generated content

The App generates motivational quote-card images, captions, and short videos that are intended for publishing on the connected Facebook page. These are produced server-side from text prompts written by the page's content team. No personal data of any external person is included in generated content.

2. How We Use Information

Stored access tokens and page identifiers are used solely to:

  • Authenticate API calls to Meta Graph API on behalf of the authorized page administrator
  • Publish content (photos with captions) to the connected page, either immediately or via Facebook's native scheduled-publish feature
  • Read public engagement metrics on previously published posts (likes, reach) for the content team's internal reporting
  • Display a connection status indicator ("Connected to [page name]") in the team's internal dashboard

3. How We Store Information

Access tokens and configuration data are stored on a private server hosted on Railway (US region). The data is not transmitted to or shared with any third party other than:

  • Meta / Facebook — to make API calls authorized by the page admin
  • Google Cloud (Vertex AI) — to generate quote-card background imagery via Imagen. Only the textual content prompt is sent; no Facebook data is transmitted to Google

No information collected by the App is sold, rented, or shared with advertising networks, analytics providers, or any other third party.

4. Data Retention

Access tokens are retained until the authorizing administrator revokes them or until the App is uninstalled from the Facebook page. Published post records and generated content are retained indefinitely for the team's content archive, unless the administrator requests deletion.

5. Your Rights

Authorized page administrators may at any time:

  • Revoke the App's access from the Facebook page's settings (Page Settings → Business Integrations)
  • Request deletion of any stored tokens or content records by contacting the address below — see also Data Deletion Instructions

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated to all authorized administrators of the App.
